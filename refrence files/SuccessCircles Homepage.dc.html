Read the full FAQ

How often do I get a new buddy?

Buddies are assigned bi-monthly, with a maximum of two cycles — four weeks — with the same partner. Fresh feedback from different peers is what breaks the plateau.

How do you know my buddy is the right fit?

We match on goals and strengths: if another member is strong where you want to grow, or has already reached the goal you're aiming at, we pair you. Members rate each experience and can request a specific buddy.

Why peers instead of a coach?

Because a peer is on the same playing field. You stay autonomous in your decisions, and you contribute as much as you receive. Research points to peer accountability as the strongest driver of performance.

What if it doesn't work for me?

You have a 30-day trial — two partnership cycles. Unhappy with your results for any reason, and we refund your investment.
